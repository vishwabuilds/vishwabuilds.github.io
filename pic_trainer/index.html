External peripherals (outside the trainer kit)
   
Add:

   
Stepper: plugs into J13 or J16 (J16 uses MOT2SEQ); wire MOT1SEQ pins 1–4 to RC4–RC7. DC motor (only one): connects itself when added, with cables on the board: GND to the board GND pad, +12V to the board’s +12V pad (left of the LED interface) and PWM to RC2 (CCP1). Set RC2 as an output, drive it (CCP1/Timer2 PWM or plain high) and switch SW1 (AC/DC 12V, top right of the board) to ON. LED strip (only one): connects itself to PORTD (RD0–RD7) with a ribbon of cables; set TRISD = 0x00 and write PORTD. Nixie tube (only one): connects itself with a ribbon of cables: RE0–RE2 to digits 0–2, RC0–RC6 to digits 3–9 and RC7 to the decimal point. Clear TRISE and TRISC (and write ADCON1 = 0x06 so RE0–RE2 are digital), then drive a pin HIGH to light its digit. ESP8266 (only one): its TX goes to RC7 (USART RX) and its RX to RC6 (USART TX) with cables on the board, and a Virtual Terminal opens above this panel. Cables and their wires disappear when the peripheral is removed.
